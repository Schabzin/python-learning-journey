How to register for 2027

Come and visit us with your child between 08:00 and 12:00 on any weekday. Bring your child's birth certificate, clinic card and both parents' ID documents. We will show you the classroom, introduce the teacher and answer your questions about fees, meals and transport.

Places are given in the order that complete forms are received. Returning children must confirm their place by 30 November. After that date, open places are offered to new families on the waiting list.
